    How the analyst works

    Each pick weighs the player's booking rate, expected minutes, the opponent they'll be up against, the referee, and how the match is likely to play out. Teams without the ball foul more, and derbies and relegation fights run hot.

    Each pick's chance is turned into a fair price. Take at is the lowest Sportsbet price that still gives at least a 5% edge. If Sportsbet is offering less, skip the bet. Stakes are quarter Kelly on a 100-unit bank, capped at 3 units, and go up when you enter a bigger price.

    • Picks are refreshed every morning (Sydney time) with the latest team news, injuries and referee appointments from Sky Sports, club accounts and journalists on X, and football stats sites.
    • Tipsters' booking picks (Paddy Power, Betfair, bet365, Oddschecker, Racing Post and card tipsters on X) are checked too. When a tipster backs the same player, the pick says so. Tipsters are a second opinion, not a reason to bet on their own.
    • About 45 minutes before each kick-off, once lineups are out, the picks for that game are checked again. Benched players are dropped and the game is marked "Lineups confirmed".
    • Lineups land about an hour before kick-off. If your player is on the bench, think twice: a sub has far fewer minutes to get booked, though the bet stands if they come on.

    Sportsbet settlement

    • A yellow or red card shown to the player counts.
    • If the player takes no part in the match, the bet is void and your stake comes back. A player who comes off the bench counts as playing.
    • Cards shown to players who aren't on the pitch, and cards after the full-time whistle, normally don't count. Check the market terms on Sportsbet before betting.
