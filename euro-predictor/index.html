League strength

How strong each domestic league is, worked out from how its clubs do in Europe. A gap of 0.10 is worth about 10% more goals for the stronger league's clubs. Clubs is how many of its clubs have played in Europe in the last 15 months: a league seen through one or two clubs is really those clubs' form.

Club ratings

Goals each club would expect to score and concede in a game against an average side from across Europe's leagues, from this season's league and European games plus last season's European games.
